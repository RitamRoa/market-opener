8. InterGlobe Aviation Ltd — Positive
   InterGlobe Aviation, IndiGo’s operator, raised charges for domestic ancillary services, including infant travel, excess baggage and priority check-in and boarding. Higher ancillary fees drop straight to operating EBITDA with zero incremental fleet operating expenditure.
